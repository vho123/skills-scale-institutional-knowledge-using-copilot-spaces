# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

OctoAcme uses a structured, customer-first approach to project management that emphasizes:

- **Customer-first delivery** – Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery** – Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership** – Each project has a named Project Manager and Product Lead
- **Data-informed decisions** – Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety** – Encourage feedback and continuous learning

This folder contains comprehensive guides for each phase of the project lifecycle, helping team members and stakeholders understand how OctoAcme manages projects from conception through release and continuous improvement.

## Project Lifecycle

OctoAcme follows a five-phase project lifecycle:

1. **Initiation** – Validate business need, align stakeholders, and decide go/no-go for planning
2. **Planning** – Break work into actionable increments, identify risks and dependencies
3. **Execution & Tracking** – Manage day-to-day delivery, track progress, and escalate blockers
4. **Release & Deployment** – Standardize releases, manage rollback, and announce to stakeholders
5. **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement** – Capture learnings and drive iterative improvements

## Core Roles

- **Project Manager (PM)** – Coordinates delivery, manages schedules, risks, and communications
- **Product Manager (PdM)** – Defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, and measures success
- **Developers** – Implement features, collaborate on design and testability
- **QA/Testing** – Validate quality and acceptance criteria
- **Stakeholders** – Provide inputs, feedback, and approvals

For detailed role descriptions and responsibilities, see [OctoAcme Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md).

## Key Artifacts

Every OctoAcme project maintains these key artifacts:

- **Project Charter / One-pager** – Problem statement, goals, success metrics, stakeholders, and timeline
- **Roadmap and Release Plan** – Milestones, delivery timeline, and major phases
- **Sprint/Iteration Backlog** – Prioritized work items with acceptance criteria
- **Definition of Done (DoD)** – Shared quality and completeness standards
- **Risk Register** – Identified risks, impact, likelihood, and mitigation plans
- **Retrospective notes and action items** – Learnings captured and follow-up improvements

## Communication Cadence

- **Daily standups** (15 min) – Progress, blockers, dependencies
- **Weekly PM + PdM sync** – Alignment on delivery and priorities
- **Twice-weekly delivery team syncs** – Execution and tactical coordination
- **Monthly stakeholder updates** – High-level status and announcements
- **Ad-hoc escalations** – As needed for risks or blockers
